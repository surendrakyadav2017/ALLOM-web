Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Kya mujhe apna password dena hoga?

Zaroori nahi. Jahan platform allow karta hai, hum staff ya user-role access par kaam karte hain. Aap use kabhi bhi hata sakte hain, aur access ki baat agreement mein likhit hoti hai.

Kya aap booking par commission lete hain?

Nahi. Hum fixed service fee par kaam karte hain. Aapki property ke hisaab se details audit ke baad batate hain.

Kya mujhe koi software lena padega?

Chhoti property mein aksar nahi. Agar platforms ya rooms zyada hon aur availability sync karni ho, to hum channel manager recommend karte hain. Uska subscription aapke naam par hota hai.

Kya ALL-OM.in ki service se meri bookings badhengi?

Hamara poora focus aapki online visibility aur bookings badhane par hai. Behtar listings, sahi samay par sahi rates, reviews ke jawab aur mazboot Google presence se aapki property zyada guests tak pahunchti hai, aur yahi bookings aur business badhane ka bharosemand tarika hai. Har mahine ki report mein aap khud dekhte hain ki bookings, room nights aur platform-wise results mein kya badla. Final nateeja season, location aur demand par bhi nirbhar karta hai, isliye hum kisi fixed number ka vaada nahi karte, par mehnat aur result dono aapko saaf dikhate hain.
